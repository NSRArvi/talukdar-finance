Answers & Clarity

Questions Before You Get Started?

Understand how Talukdar Finance works with you.

We assist with residential home loans (first home buyer, upgrading, and refinancing), car and vehicle finance, personal loans for major planned commitments, and commercial equipment and business finance.
The process starts with an initial discussion to clarify your objectives. Next, we review available options from our lender panel. Once you select a preferred pathway, we assist you in compiling necessary documentation and submit the application to the lender for assessment.
Typically, lenders require proof of identity, recent income evidence (payslips, tax returns, or notices of assessment), bank statements reflecting living expenses, and details of existing assets and liabilities. We supply an easy checklist tailored to your situation.
Absolutely. An obligation-free initial consultation allows you to review your borrowing capacity and understand policy nuances before lodging any formal credit inquiry that could impact your credit file.
In most standard residential lending scenarios, our brokerage services are paid directly via commission by the chosen lender upon settlement. Any specific brokerage fee or commercial advisory costs will always be transparently outlined in your Credit Quote prior to engagement.
You can fill out the consultation form below, call our direct line, or send an email. We arrange meetings via phone, secure video conference, or in person at our offices at a time convenient for you.
